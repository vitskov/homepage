Software

Our software is hosted on GitHub, mostly in the nebneuron repository; newer packages are under my GitHub account.

  • topological data analysis
  • combinatorial neural codes
  • threshold-linear networks

Topological data analysis

  • Hodge-Laplacians Rust / Python · 2025 Weighted complexes of sequences and the spectra of their Hodge Laplacians, at scale.
  • Simplicial.jl Julia · 2016 Data structures and algorithms for filtered simplicial complexes, directed complexes and combinatorial codes.
  • CliqueTop MATLAB · 2013 Clique topology of symmetric matrices, e.g. neural correlations: detects geometric structure invariant to monotone transformations.

Combinatorial neural codes

  • combinatorial_codes Python · 2025 Combinatorial codes and obstructions to convexity.
  • neural-ideal MATLAB · 2013 Canonical form of the neural ideal of a code (the neural ring).

Threshold-linear networks

  • Neural Choreography web app · 2026 Interactive CTLN visualizer by Rob Ghrist (UPenn): firing rates and trajectories in state space, in the browser.
  • CTLN Basic 2.0 MATLAB · 2023 Simulations of combinatorial threshold-linear networks; reproduces the figures of Morrison, Degeratu, Itskov, Curto (2024).
